About Me

JB

Blending Technology with Human-Centered Design

As an aspiring designer with a unique background in Computer Science and Cognitive Science at Occidental College, I bring a distinctive perspective to digital design. My work exists at the intersection of technology and human cognition—creating experiences that are not only visually stunning but deeply intuitive.

I believe great design transcends aesthetics. It's about understanding how people think, feel, and interact. Every pixel I place, every interaction I craft, is informed by a deep understanding of human behavior and a relentless pursuit of elegance in simplicity.
